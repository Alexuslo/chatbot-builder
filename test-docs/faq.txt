# FAQ: FitTrack Fitness App

## General Questions

### What is FitTrack?
FitTrack is a mobile fitness application that helps you track workouts, nutrition, and health goals. Available on iOS and Android.

### Is FitTrack free?
FitTrack offers a free tier with basic features. FitTrack Pro ($9.99/month or $79.99/year) unlocks all features including advanced analytics, personalized plans, and priority support.

### How do I create an account?
Download the app, tap "Sign Up", enter your email and password, or sign up with Google/Apple. You'll need to verify your email address.

## Workout Features

### How does the workout tracker work?
Log your exercises by searching our database of 500+ exercises. Track sets, reps, and weights. FitTrack automatically calculates volume and suggests progressive overload.

### Can I create custom workouts?
Yes! Go to My Workouts → Create New. Add exercises, set reps and weights, and save as a template for future use.

### Does FitTrack have workout plans?
Yes. We offer 50+ pre-built plans for different goals:
- Weight Loss (4-12 week programs)
- Muscle Building (8-16 week programs)
- Strength Training (6-12 week programs)
- Cardio & Endurance (4-8 week programs)

### Can I track cardio?
Yes. FitTrack tracks running, cycling, swimming, and 30+ other cardio activities. Use GPS for outdoor activities or manually log duration and intensity.

## Nutrition Features

### How does the calorie tracker work?
Search our database of 200,000+ foods or scan barcodes. Log meals and track macros (protein, carbs, fat). Set daily goals based on your objectives.

### Can I create meal plans?
Yes. Go to Nutrition → Meal Planner. Drag and drop foods to create daily or weekly meal plans. Export as PDF or share with friends.

### Does FitTrack sync with MyFitnessPal?
Yes. Go to Settings → Integrations → MyFitnessPal. Log in to sync food data between apps.

## Account & Settings

### How do I upgrade to Pro?
Go to Settings → Subscription → Upgrade. Choose monthly ($9.99) or yearly ($79.99). Payment is processed through App Store or Google Play.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings → Subscription → Manage. You can cancel anytime. Access continues until the end of the billing period.

### Can I export my data?
Yes. Go to Settings → Data → Export. Download your data as CSV or JSON. Available for Pro users only.

### How do I contact support?
- Email: support@fittrack.example.com
- In-app chat: Settings → Help → Chat with us
- Response time: Within 24 hours (Pro users: within 4 hours)

## Privacy & Security

### Is my data secure?
Yes. We use AES-256 encryption. Data is stored on secure AWS servers. We never sell your personal data.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

### Do you share data with third parties?
Only with your explicit consent. We share anonymized, aggregated data for research purposes. Never personally identifiable information.
